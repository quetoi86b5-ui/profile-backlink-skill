# Platform Pool — Business Profile Registration

Reusable candidate pool distilled from the Diamond Rise run (100 profiles, Nov 2026). For a new business, work the WORKS list top-down in 2-site browser sessions, run VERIFY-FIRST before creating anything, and never spend time on the DO-NOT-RETRY list unless conditions change. Outcomes assume: company Google account for SSO, free plans only, ~5-minute time-boxes, 4-minute cap on credential waits.

## B. VERIFY-FIRST — often already exist; check before creating

- Official socials: Facebook Page, Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube channel, Threads (usually mirrors Instagram), LinkedIn personal profiles (a Company Page needs a personal admin — owner-only).
- Business data records: local tax registry (Masothue in VN), Opencorporates, Dun & Bradstreet (fixes route through the local partner), Cybo, local data sites (doanhnghiep.biz, infocom.vn, vnbis.com in VN — check legal-representative accuracy, cross-contamination between similar companies happens).
- Maps & POI: Google Business Profile (request ownership from the company account; self-claim opens after a waiting period), Bing Places (claim, then owner verifies by PIN), Apple Maps / Apple Business Connect (claim needs owner's Apple ID), HERE WeGo, Waze (mirrors Google), TomTom (submit POI via Map Share), Foursquare (create the listing free; claiming costs a one-time fee — do not pay unprompted).
- Review platforms: Trustpilot (business page pre-exists unclaimed; activation email goes to the domain mailbox), SmartCustomer/Sitejabber (pre-exists unclaimed).
- Dev/Q&A profiles: Qiita and StockTwits both pre-existed for Diamond Rise (created months earlier, with slightly stale address data) — always fetch the guessed public URL before registering.

## D. Wave 7 lessons (Nov 2026, from a 395-row third-party list)

- **Password transplant does NOT work.** The Secure Vault / credential service cannot copy a saved password onto a brand-new site's signup form. Every email+password site surfaces a secure-entry card and the owner must type the password (the owner prefers reusing the SAME company password everywhere) into that site's card exactly once; it is then saved for that site. Budget sessions around the owner being present; a card left unfilled for ~4 minutes parks the site, ~2 hours kills the session.
- **Verify the public URL yourself, always.** Sessions have reported success for profiles that were 404 (Teletype's first pass) and have died mid-run leaving finished profiles behind (SlideServe, Linktree). Fetch or read-only-browse every claimed URL before counting it.
- **Template/AI content is a liability.** Site builders' AI templates insert invented prices and testimonials (Strikingly) — audit the live page and strip anything not in the locked entity data before leaving it published.
- **Username collisions happen.** Pinterest @diamondrise belongs to a stranger — never touch it; fall back to the next username in the locked chain.
